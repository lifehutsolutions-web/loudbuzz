Frequently asked questions

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள்

What is LoudBuzz?

LoudBuzz is a QR-code photo booth for your shop. Customers scan a QR code, take a selfie or short reel inside a frame designed for your brand, save it to their phone and post it on Instagram, tagging your business.

Do my customers need to install an app? (App install பண்ணணுமா?)

No. The booth opens in the phone browser (Chrome or Safari) as soon as the QR code is scanned. Customers only need to allow camera access.

Who designs the photo frame?

We design the frame for your brand, using your logo, colours and tagline, so every photo and reel carries your identity.

Can customers record reels with sound?

Yes. Customers can record a short reel with the sound on or off, and save it straight to their phone, ready to upload to Instagram.

How do customers get a reward?

You decide the reward, for example a discount or a free gift. Customers show their Instagram post with your tag at the counter to claim it.

Which businesses can use LoudBuzz?

Any business with walk-in customers: clothing and gift shops, cafés and restaurants, salons, gyms, showrooms, events and exhibitions across Chennai, Tamil Nadu and the rest of India.

How much does it cost? (எவ்வளவு செலவாகும்?)

It is a simple one-time setup priced about like a pizza. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share the exact details for your business.
